# App Features

Here is the comprehensive list of features currently available in the app, categorized by domain:

## 1. Calculate
- **Percentage Calculator**: Find percentages, ratios & percentage change
- **Scientific Calculator**: Full scientific keypad (`sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `ln`, `log`, `sqrt`, `^`, `π`, `e`, DEG/RAD, history)
- **Equation Solver**: Linear ($ax+b=0$), Quadratic ($ax^2+bx+c=0$ with real & complex roots), and 2×2 Simultaneous Linear Systems (Cramer's rule)
- **Matrix Calculator**: 2×2 and 3×3 matrix addition, subtraction, multiplication, determinant, transpose & inverse
- **GCD & LCM Calculator**: Greatest Common Divisor & Least Common Multiple for 2 or multiple numbers with Euclidean algorithm
- **Prime Number & Factorization**: Primality test, prime factor decomposition with exponents, and next prime finder
- **Permutation & Combination**: $nPr$ and $nCr$ with step-by-step factorial formulas

## 2. Convert
- **Unit Converter**: Length, Mass, Temperature, Area, Volume, Speed, Data
- **Land Area Converter**: Acres, Cents, Hectares, Square Feet, Square Meters, Guntha, Ground, Bigha

## 3. Date & Time
- **Age**: Exact years, months, days & next birthday countdown
- **Date Difference**: Calendar days & business working days between dates
- **Duration Converter**: Hours to days, minutes & seconds breakdown

## 4. Finance
- **GST Calculator**: Exclusive & Inclusive GST with CGST and SGST
- **Loan EMI Calculator**: Monthly EMI, interest breakdown & amortization schedule
- **Discount Calculator**: Final price, multi-tier promo codes & savings
- **Currency Converter**: Live & offline rates: USD, INR, EUR, GBP, AED, JPY
- **Interest Calculator**: Simple & compound interest with compounding frequencies
- **Tip & Split Bill**: Calculate restaurant tips & split bills among people

## 5. Health & Fitness
- **Health & Fitness Suite**:
  - [x] **Body-fat estimator**: US Navy method using neck, waist, hip, and height with fitness category classifications
  - [x] **Waist-to-height ratio (WHtR)**: Abdominal visceral fat risk gauge and health boundaries
  - [x] **Running pace and speed calculator**: Pace (min/km, min/mi), speed (km/h, mph), and race split forecasts (5K, 10K, Half, Full Marathon)
  - [x] **Step-to-distance calculator**: Step count to km, miles, burned calories, and height-calibrated stride length
  - [x] **Heart-rate zone calculator**: Tanaka max HR & Karvonen reserve formulas across 5 training zones (Warm-up to VO2 Max)
  - [x] **Workout interval timer**: HIIT & Tabata timer with customizable work/rest cycles, sets, rounds, and audio/haptic cues
  - [x] **Protein and macronutrient calculator**: TDEE baseline, fitness goals (cutting, maintenance, bulking), and grams/calories of Protein, Carbs & Fats
  - [x] **Water intake tracker**: Daily hydration target by body weight, quick +250ml/+500ml/+750ml logging, circular progress ring & daily log history
- **Health Calculator**: BMI, BMR, ideal body weight & daily calorie requirements

## 6. Education & Academic
- **Academic & Education Suite**:
  - [x] **Marks percentage calculator**: Dynamic subject list with maximum marks, scored marks, aggregate %, letter grade & pass/fail status
  - [x] **Exam score needed calculator**: Target final grade, current coursework grade, and final exam weighting
  - [x] **Attendance eligibility calculator**: Attended vs total classes, minimum required attendance %, classes to attend or safe skips
  - [x] **Scientific calculator**: Full scientific keypad with trigonometric, logarithmic, exponential, powers & history
  - [x] **Equation solver**: Linear equations, quadratic equations with discriminant & real/complex roots, 2×2 linear system solver
  - [x] **Prime number and factorization tools**: Primality test, prime factor tree, next prime finder, and prime numbers generator
  - [x] **GCD and LCM calculator**: Multi-number greatest common divisor and least common multiple
  - [x] **Matrix calculator**: 2×2 and 3×3 matrix arithmetic, determinants, inverses, and transposes
  - [x] **Permutation and combination calculator**: $nPr$ and $nCr$ with step-by-step factorial breakdowns
- **GPA & CGPA Calculator**: Course grade points, weighted credits & percentage scale

## 7. Home & Travel
- **Home, Construction & Travel Suite**:
  - [x] **Fuel mileage and trip cost**: Distance, vehicle fuel economy, fuel cost per unit, total fuel needed & cost per passenger
  - [x] **EV charging cost estimator**: Battery capacity (kWh), start/target charge %, electricity rate, charger efficiency & charge time
  - [x] **Paint quantity calculator**: Room length, width, height, deductions for doors/windows, number of coats & paint required in Liters/Gallons
  - [x] **Tile and flooring calculator**: Room dimensions, tile dimensions, wastage % buffer, total tiles needed & box counts
  - [x] **Concrete and cement estimator**: Slab/footing length, width, thickness, mix designs (1:2:4, 1:1.5:3, 1:3:6), 50kg cement bags, sand & gravel volume
  - [x] **Land area converter**: Instant cross-conversion for Acres, Cents, Hectares, Square Feet, Square Meters, Guntha, Ground, Bigha
  - [x] **Water tank capacity**: Rectangular and cylindrical water tank volume in Liters, US Gallons, and Cubic Meters
  - [x] **Appliance electricity consumption**: Appliance wattage (Watts), daily hours used, electricity rate -> daily, monthly, and annual costs
  - [x] **Travel budget splitter**: Group expense ledger with payer tracking and automated minimal debt settlements
  - [x] **Download-time estimator**: File size (MB, GB, TB) and network speed (Mbps, MB/s, Gbps) -> download time in days, hours, minutes, seconds
- **Electricity Bill**: Estimate monthly power cost by units or home appliances

## 8. Files, Text & Media Tools
- **Advanced PDF Toolkit**:
  - [x] **PDF merge, split and compress**:
    - Merge multiple PDFs with page order management
    - Split PDF by page range (e.g., 1-3, 5, 8-10)
    - Extract, reorder, rotate (90°/180°/270°), and delete pages
    - Compress PDF with stream compression
    - Add password protection & AES-256 encryption with permission controls
  - [x] **PDF to images**: High-resolution PNG image export
  - Signatures, stamps, annotations, interactive form fill, PDF to text, Images to PDF, Text to PDF
- **Image Toolkit**:
  - [x] **Image compression and format conversion**: Quality slider, target sizes, JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, GIF conversion
  - Resize & crop with aspect ratios (1:1, 16:9, 4:3, 9:16)
  - Rotate (90°, 180°, 270°) and flip (horizontal & vertical mirror)
  - Combine & stitch images (horizontal, vertical, 2×2 grid)
  - Image to Base64 and Base64 to image
  - Remove EXIF metadata (GPS, camera info, timestamps)
  - Text watermarks & passport-size photo sheets
- **Image Collage Maker**:
  - [x] **Image collage maker**: Pick 2 to 9 photos, select grid layout (2×1, 1×2, 2×2, 3×1, 3×2, 3×3), customize border spacing & background colors, export high-res JPG/PNG
- **Audio & Video Studio**:
  - [x] **Audio trimmer**: Visual waveform cutter, start/end timecode sliders, duration calculation & clip export
  - [x] **Video compressor and trimmer**: Bitrate estimation, resolution presets (480p, 720p, 1080p), duration trimmer & compression ratio planning
- **Text Diff & Cleaner**:
  - [x] **Text comparison and difference highlighting**: Line-by-line diff comparing original vs modified text with color-coded additions (+), deletions (-), and unified diff copy
  - [x] **Duplicate line remover**: Duplicate line cleaner, whitespace trimming, blank line removal, case sensitivity toggle, and alphabetical/length sorting
- **Markdown Editor & Preview**:
  - [x] **Markdown editor and preview**: Live editor with rich syntax toolbar (H1-H3, Bold, Italic, Code, Blockquote, Lists, Tasks, Tables, Links), live rendered preview & `.md` export
- **File & ZIP Suite**:
  - [x] **ZIP and archive utilities**:
    - ZIP files and folders into custom archives with compression statistics
    - Extract & inspect ZIP archives and share individual files
    - Batch file renaming (prefix, suffix, find/replace, sequential numbering, case conversions)
    - File size analyzer with storage breakdown progress bars
    - File hash calculator (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512) with verification
    - Duplicate file finder via cryptographic byte checksums
    - File metadata & magic header viewer
    - CSV ⇄ JSON two-way converter with formatted indentation
- **OCR & Voice Studio**:
  - On-device photo & document text extraction
  - Screenshot text copy with chip selectors
  - Image tables to structured CSV conversion
  - Smart contact and entity extractor (emails, phone numbers, URLs, dates, amounts)
  - Text-to-speech (TTS) & Speech-to-text (STT) voice dictation
  - OCR recognized text to PDF
- **Document Scanner**: Camera document scans, B&W filters, ID card mode & Signature pad
- **Text & Words Analyzer**: Word count, character count & case transformation tools

## 9. Developer & QR Suite
- **QR & Barcode Suite**:
  - [x] **QR code generator and scanner**:
    - Real-time QR & barcode scanner using camera with flashlight & flip
    - Scan QR / barcode from image files in gallery
    - Generate custom QR codes with colors, error correction, and logo
    - Wi-Fi sharing QR generator (WPA/WPA2/WEP/Open, SSID, hidden network)
    - Contact & website QR generator (vCard, URL, Email, Phone, SMS, Geolocation)
    - Scan and generate 1D & 2D barcodes (Code 128, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, Code 39, ISBN, Aztec, PDF417)
    - Save & share QR codes as high-res PNG images or printable PDFs
    - Persistent QR scan history with timestamps
    - Bulk QR code generator with batch export to printable PDF sheets
- **XML & YAML Formatter**:
  - [x] **XML and YAML formatter**: Prettify, validate, format, and minify XML and YAML/JSON with syntax error reporting
- **Device & Hardware Studio**:
  - [x] **Device information viewer**: Model, brand, manufacturer, OS version, API/build level, display resolution, DPR, hardware architecture & one-tap spec report export
  - [x] **Compass and spirit level**: Digital rotating magnetic compass with cardinal headings, plus 2D circular bubble surface level & pitch/roll inclinometer with zero-calibration
  - [x] **Sensor tester**: Real-time diagnostic stream for 3-axis Accelerometer ($m/s^2$ and total $g$), Gyroscope ($rad/s$), Magnetometer ($\mu T$), and interactive shake motion detector
- **Developer Suite**: JSON Formatter/Minifier, Base64, UUID Generator, Color codes (HEX/RGB) & URL Encoder
